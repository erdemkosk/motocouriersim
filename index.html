01 · Weather

The weather turns, the road changes.

Asphalt shines in the rain, tyres leave tracks in the snow. The bike and the rider get soaked and snowed on.

Eight kinds of weather, and each one changes the ride

  • Five layers of rain sound from drizzle to downpour; in a storm, lightning branches across the sky and the thunder comes from where it struck.
  • Snow builds up: roads, roofs and parked cars turn white, and tyres and footsteps leave tracks.
  • Grip drops on wet and snowy roads. Water fans out of puddles, snow puffs into clouds, drops run down your visor.
  • It stays dry under viaducts and awnings. Morning fog turns headlights into beams, and on rainy nights the city lights reflect off the road.

Vertical

Snow changes the city

Tyre tracks, footprints, snow piling up on the seat. Shot as if for your phone, and all of it is the game itself.

02 · World

The island wasn't enough. Calvera County waits across the bridge.

Leave San Verano behind: villas, tin-roofed hillsides and a long highway are waiting.

San Verano + Calvera County

  • A highway ring around the island city of San Verano: 12 city exits, bridged interchanges and tunnels.
  • Nine new neighbourhoods: the villas of Bel Oro and Malvista Heights, and hillside and shanty districts like Slopeton, Brinewell and Tincan Row.
  • Two new bridges, roadside petrol stations and charging points, and neighbourhood streets with their own traffic.
  • City bus: walk to a stop, pick your destination on the map and have your bike brought along.

Living details

  • Power poles, high-voltage lines and birds on the wires; strings of lights over the bazaar.
  • Nature sounds from their sources: birds in the trees, crickets at night, frogs by the stream, a dog in a garden.
  • Grass, stones and twigs that change by region. Faint moonlit shadows at night, glowing clouds at sunset.
  • Life in the distance too: traffic flowing up to 800 metres away, crowds on the pavements.

03 · Bikes

Petrol or plug-in, your choice.

From a 110 cc scooter to a 1250 cc adventure bike, from a chopper to an electric delivery bike.

Corva Volt: quiet and electric

  • Charging points at every petrol station and in the neighbourhoods. Grab the cable yourself and plug it into the bike.
  • Start and stop charging from the Volt Charge app on your phone; you pay for what goes into the battery.
  • Regenerative braking: let off the throttle and the bike slows itself while the battery charges.
  • You can start the story on the Volt too: pick your first bike from three.

Riding feel, rewritten

  • Lean-to-turn, counter-steering, weight transfer under braking; in hard corners your knee touches down.
  • Aquaplaning and highsides in the wet, sliding on grass; manual gears and engine braking.
  • Real hands in first person: fingers on the brake and clutch levers, wrists that turn with the bars.
  • Every bike has its own dash, sound and character.

04 · People

Every face in the city is different.

Realistic characters and a new wardrobe. Build your own courier in the character creator.

Ercan, Sertaç and Sign Night

You start out as a courier working for Ercan. Then things get bigger: jobs picked up in front of shops, tails, chases, and Sertaç, who wants to take over the city. The story ends on Sign Night.

On top of that, 12 side stories and 30 missions, each with its own people and its own streets.

The game goes on when you get off the bike

  • Run, crouch, vault obstacles, hang from a wall and climb up.
  • Street fights: jabs, hooks, guard and parries. A boxing gym and a night ring.
  • Basketball with whoever's on the court: 1-on-1 or H-O-R-S-E, with a crowd and bets.
  • Steal cars with lockpicking and hot-wiring mini-games; raise your wanted level and the police come after you.

05 · Courier

The job is still the delivery.

Grab the right bag at the shop, run to your bike, hand it over at the door.

Everything in one shift

  • Courier, moto taxi, yellow cab, warehouse, kitchen or mechanic: whatever work you want that day.
  • Repair mode in the garage: strip a part, fit a new one, lift the bike on the ramp.
  • Start your own courier company: buy bikes, hire staff, hit the daily target.
  • Daily courier pack: a new reward on the main menu every day.

Ride together

  • Online co-op for up to 4 players through Steam: one shared world, one shared store, one shared wallet.
  • Proximity voice chat with voice changers, pings, quick chat and pillion rides.
  • Team ranks, daily goals and duels – and plenty of chaos.
